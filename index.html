Frequently asked questions

What is the Goethe-Zertifikat B2?

The Goethe-Zertifikat B2 certifies upper-intermediate German. It proves you can understand complex texts, participate in discussions on abstract topics, and write clear, detailed arguments. It's widely required for university admission in Germany, regulated professions (nursing, engineering), and skilled worker migration visas.

How long is the Goethe B2 exam?

The written portion takes approximately 3 hours: reading (65 min), listening (40 min), and writing (75 min). The speaking module adds about 15 minutes, conducted in pairs. Total exam time is roughly 3 hours and 15 minutes.

What score do I need to pass?

You need at least 60 out of 100 points overall. Each of the four modules is scored independently, and you must pass each one. Failed modules can be retaken individually.

How does the AI writing review work?

Write your essay or formal text, then receive instant analysis against B2 scoring criteria. The AI checks argumentation structure, grammatical range and accuracy, vocabulary sophistication, and task fulfilment — providing specific, actionable feedback.
